# Multidimensional Perfectionism Questionnaire An HTML questionnaire for educational or research use. It presents 45 items, uses a 1–7 Likert scale, and calculates scores for three subscales: - Self-oriented perfectionism - Other-oriented perfectionism - Socially prescribed perfectionism ## How to use 1. Open `index.html` in a browser. 2. Or publish the files on GitHub Pages. ## Files - `index.html` - `styles.css` - `script.js` ## Source Based on the Multidimensional Perfectionism Scale by: - Hewitt, P. L. & Flett, G. L. (1991). *Perfectionism in the self and social contexts: Conceptualization, assessment, and association with psychopathology.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 60, 456-470. - Hewitt, P. L., Flett, G. L., Turnbull-Donovan, W., & Mikail, S. F. (1991). *The Multidimensional Perfectionism Scale: Reliability, validity, and psychometric properties in psychiatric samples.* Psychological Assessment, 3, 464-468. - Hewitt, P. L. & Flett, G. L. (2004). *Multidimensional Perfectionism Scale: Technical Manual.* Multihealth Systems Inc. ## Note The original scale was provided for research use only. Check permissions before using it outside research.
